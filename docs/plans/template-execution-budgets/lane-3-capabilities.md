## Data and helper contract

InputOptions defines positive bytes/items/steps/depth allowances. Typed method-free scalars, arrays/slices and string-keyed maps retain their types, nil and zero-value behavior. Interfaces unwrap recursively; active identities reject cycles while repeated acyclic children charge each copied occurrence. Collection storage and payload are reserved before allocation. The logical schedule excludes fixed control objects and allocator overhead. Strings share immutable storage. Snapshotting cannot protect a Go map that the caller mutates concurrently during admission.

BuiltinBinding is opaque and immutable. LookupBuiltin selects the accepted private identity; BuiltinAliases copies a finite alias map at construction. There is no RegisterFunc-based approval and no function-name/program-counter trust. Supported defaults and explicit exclusions are listed in the public contract. Karsto helpers absent from this registry (including join/formatNumber/time helpers and three-argument replace) need their own bounded owning-layer implementations. Lane 4 must not silently change arities/outputs or approve the old callbacks.
